DeepL is highly accurate for European languages and consistently ranks among the top MT engines for language pairs like English, German, French, and Spanish but accuracy drops meaningfully outside those strengths. Performance weakens for non-European languages, low-resource language pairs, and highly technical or domain-specific content. According to an Intento benchmark , DeepL ranked as the top-performing engine in 65% of language pairs tested, with the advantage concentrated in European combinations.
