A translation style guide is a reference document that defines how your brand should sound in every target language. It sets tone, formality, grammar rules, formatting, approved terminology, and do-not-translate terms for human translators and AI translation alike. Without one, the same product name can appear three different ways across markets, or a casual English voice can turn unintentionally formal in German.
