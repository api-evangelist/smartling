Continuous localization is the practice of translating software and content automatically as strings change, integrated directly into a CI/CD pipeline instead of handled as a separate, batched project. Choosing the right tool in 2026 comes down to five decisions: how it connects to your codebase, how it reuses past translations, how it enforces quality, how it scales across languages, and how it prices at volume. This guide walks through each so a product, engineering, or localization lead can evaluate vendors against real criteria rather than feature checklists.
