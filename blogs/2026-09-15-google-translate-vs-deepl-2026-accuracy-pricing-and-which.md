Neither Google Translate nor DeepL is universally better so the right choice depends on your language pairs, content type, and quality requirements. DeepL consistently outperforms Google for European languages and nuanced business content; Google leads on breadth, supporting over 130 languages compared to DeepL's more limited coverage. According to an Intento benchmark , DeepL ranked as the top-performing engine in 65% of language pairs tested, with particular strength in European combinations.
