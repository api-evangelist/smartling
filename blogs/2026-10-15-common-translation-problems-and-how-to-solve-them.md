The most common translation problems stem from structural language differences, idioms, cultural references, and words with multiple meanings. Grammar and word order vary dramatically between languages, English follows subject-verb-object order while Farsi uses subject-object-verb, forcing translators to rearrange entire sentences to sound natural. Idiomatic expressions like "kick the bucket" cannot be translated literally and require deep cultural knowledge to convey equivalent meaning.
