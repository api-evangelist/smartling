Reusing an inherited vendor evaluation when shopping for translation providers is standard practice, but it’s likely time to update that process. Call it a request for proposal (RFP), a vendor comparison, or a renewal review: it's the document you send to providers asking how they'd run your translation work and what it would cost, and starting from the last one saves time.
