To translate a Google Doc, click "Tools," then "Translate document," choose your target language from the dropdown menu, and click "Translate"—the translated file opens in a new tab while preserving your original. Google Docs' built-in translation supports the languages available in Google Translate. However, Google Translate often struggles with context and accuracy, particularly for technical terms and complex sentences, and limits file size to 10 megabytes.
