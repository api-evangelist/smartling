Website translation technology falls into five main types: multilingual CMS integrations, AI and machine translation, machine translation post-editing (MTPE), translation memory (TM), and translation proxies. The right combination depends on your site's architecture, content volume, and publishing frequency. The choice matters because most of the web is concentrated in a few languages: according to W3Techs (October 2026), just 14 languages account for over 90% of website content, and English alone accounts for 49.5%.
